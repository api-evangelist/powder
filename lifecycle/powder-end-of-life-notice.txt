Dear Powder community,


After 7 incredible years together, our journey with Powder has reached its end. We've stretched our financial capacities as far as possible, but we can no longer sustain the app.


Starting today, the Powder app will no longer be updated or maintained. All active subscriptions are being cancelled so they won't renew. For those on yearly plans, we're doing our best to refund the unused portion of your subscription wherever possible - though unfortunately, we may not be able to make it work in every case. If you're impacted, please accept our sincere apologies.


More importantly, we want to say thank you.


To every subscriber: you gave us the means to keep building.


To every user: you made Powder what it was - creating millions of clips, memories, and moments that captured the joy of gaming and streaming.


To our Discord team and community: you were our heart. Thank you for the energy, kindness, and creativity you brought every day.


We're deeply proud of what we built together: a tool that made creativity accessible and gaming moments sharable at scale. Powder started a community, and remained one all along.


From the bottom of our hearts: thank you for being part of this adventure.


With gratitude, The Powder team
